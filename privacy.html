RealKeyword AI News — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-16

1. Data we access

Using the youtube.readonly permission, the app reads only your YouTube subscription list (channel id, title, thumbnail URL). It does not read your videos, comments, or watch history, and has no write access.

2. How we use it

Only to fetch and show the latest videos from those channels inside the app, alongside other AI news.

3. Storage and security

All data stays on your own device. The sign-in token is encrypted by OS secure storage (Electron safeStorage); the subscription list is stored in a local file. Nothing is sent to or stored on any server we operate.

4. Sharing

We never sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties. Network calls go only to Google APIs (to read subscriptions) and YouTube public RSS feeds (to fetch videos).

5. Deletion

Press "Logout" in the app at any time to delete the stored token and subscription data from your device. You can also revoke access in your Google Account third-party access settings.
